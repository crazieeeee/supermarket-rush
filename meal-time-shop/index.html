How to play

1

Sort the food

Drag the food into the Countable or Uncountable box. You can also tap the food, then tap a box.

2

Choose the question

Look at the food. Tap the right question. We say Are there any eggs? and Is there any milk?

3

Shop check

Look at the shelf in Mint Lane Shop. Tap the right answer: Yes, there is. No, there isn't. Yes, there are. No, there aren't.

You have 3 lives. The timer gets a little faster. You can pause at any time.
